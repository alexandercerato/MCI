INDEX GUIDE — WHAT THE MCI TRACKS

A price barometer for the Middle Corridor.

The MCI is an experimental westbound freight benchmark. It condenses comparable 40-foot container quotations on selected Trans-Caspian reference lanes into one number, while keeping the underlying USD/FEU basket visible.

CURRENT READING
—MCI
—USD / FEU reference basket

Jan 2026 = 100. A reading of 116.8 means the benchmark basket is 16.8% above the January 2026 base level. It does not mean every shipment on the corridor costs 16.8% more.

01

What it tracks

The quoted cost of moving one standard 40-foot dry/HC container westbound on a basket of representative Middle Corridor lanes.

02

What enters the basket

Comparable public freight-rate ranges. The midpoint of each range is multiplied by that lane’s weight, then the weighted values are added together.

03

What moves the index

Changes in observed freight quotations on the reference lanes. Volumes, congestion, transit time and Aktau conditions provide context, but do not mechanically move the MCI unless reflected in freight prices.

04

What it does not claim

It is not a live transaction tape, a universal door-to-door quote or an average of every shipment. Taxes, insurance, demurrage and bespoke last-mile services are excluded where separable.

08 — METHODOLOGY v0.7

A benchmark, not a fabricated live price.

The automation checks for new evidence every five minutes. A new MCI fixing is created only when a verified, comparable reference-lane observation changes the basket.

01

Reference shipment

1 × 40' dry/HC container, general cargo, terminal-to-terminal, westbound, USD/FEU. Duties, VAT, insurance, detention/demurrage and bespoke last-mile services are excluded where separable.

02

Reference lanes

MC1 Xi’an–Alat 40%, MC2 Xi’an–Tbilisi 30%, MC3 Xi’an–Türkiye 30%. Weights remain provisional pending a deeper recurring quote set.

03

Aktau treatment

Aktau metrics are shown independently from the MCI unless a price observation is explicitly part of a reference-lane through-rate. Port pressure is not scored until recurring queue and dwell data are available.

04

5-minute checks

GitHub Actions is scheduled every five minutes. The browser also reloads the published data file every five minutes. If no new verified quote exists, the previous fixing is retained; no interpolation is used.
