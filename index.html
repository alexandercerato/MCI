Index methodology

What the MCFI tracks

The structural model is built only from route components that can be verified independently. A normalized MCFI value is published only when the full Xi’an-to-destination structural benchmark is supported by verified components; missing legs are shown as unavailable rather than estimated.

Methodology & sources

Standard shipment

One loaded 40' HC, general dry cargo, westbound from Xi'an through Altynkol, Aktau and Alat/Baku.

Structural benchmark

Published route and segment tariffs are kept separate by scope. Missing components are not inferred by subtraction from market prices.

Market observation

The midpoint of a comparable monthly market range is used for historical market observations; single-provider quotes are displayed separately.

Publication rule

A normalized MCFI is published only after the complete structural reference can be independently verified. Until then the historical chart remains in USD/40HC.
