Index methodology

What the MCFI tracks

The MCFI compares observed Xi’an–Alat/Absheron 40HC market freight with a transparent fundamental route-cost model. Official tariffs are used where available; modelled components are explicitly identified and never disguised as executable quotations.

MCFI history

MCFI · historical fundamental-cost series

Observed market freight divided by the period-specific fundamental cost. Official TITR anchors are used when available; post-March 2026 values carry forward the last published official anchor and are flagged accordingly.
