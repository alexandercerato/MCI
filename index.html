Index methodology

What the MCFI tracks

The MCFI combines Xi’an–Baku and Xi’an–Mersin/Ambarli market assessments with equal 50% weights. Both routes must refer to the same month. The denominator is the documented Xi’an–Baku structural cost reference; it does not yet include a separately verified Türkiye continuation cost.
