Methodology rules

  1. Ex ante definition: one loaded 40' HC, general dry cargo, westbound from Xi'an through Altynkol–Aktau–Alat/Baku.
  2. Fixed basket: 50% Xi'an→Baku and 50% Xi'an→Mersin/Ambarli. The weights do not change month to month.
  3. Route assessment: midpoint of the comparable monthly market range; median midpoint if more than one same-grade market assessment exists.
  4. Publication threshold: both core routes must be observed. Single-carrier rate cards never fill a missing market lane.
  5. No backfilling: missing months remain missing. Older non-comparable corridor tariffs are preserved in raw evidence rather than normalized into fictional MCFI values.
