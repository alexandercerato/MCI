Index methodology

What the MCFI tracks

MCFI-40 is a freight-rate benchmark in USD per loaded 40HC container for the complete Xi’an–Ambarli/Mersin route, via Kazakhstan, the Caspian Sea, Azerbaijan and Georgia. It uses the midpoint of the published full-route market range; where several comparable assessments exist for a month, it uses the median of their midpoints. Baku-only prices, provider tariffs and structural cost estimates remain separate reference data. The assessment month is always shown; refreshing the site does not create a new quote.
